## Report a problem

Include the following so support can reproduce the issue:

- The affected subdomain and your GitHub username.
- What you expected and what happened.
- The exact error message and approximate time, including your timezone.
- The steps you tried, plus relevant screenshots or DNS output.

Do not include passwords, session cookies, access tokens, or private keys.

## Report abuse

Include the full address or URL, what you observed, when you observed it, and relevant evidence. Use the abuse email for material that should not be posted publicly.

## Account settings

Open **Settings** in the dashboard for available account controls. Account deletion is a separate action from [deleting a subdomain](/manage-domains#delete-a-subdomain); read its confirmation carefully before continuing.
