isroot gives your projects an address such as `myproject.isroot.in`. Register a subdomain, then point it to your hosting provider or server.

<Callout type="info">
These docs are in beta. Dashboard messages show the result of each action; if something differs from this guide, [contact support](/support).
</Callout>

## Start here

1. [Create an account and register your subdomain](/getting-started).
2. [Connect your website or application](/connect-project).
3. [Verify your DNS settings](/troubleshooting).

Registering a subdomain reserves its name. You still need to deploy your application with a hosting provider and configure its DNS records.
