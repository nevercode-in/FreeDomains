## Create your account

1. Open [isroot.in](https://isroot.in) and choose **Get Started**.
2. Enter your name if prompted and complete the CAPTCHA.
3. Choose **Signup with GitHub** and complete the GitHub authorization flow.
4. If you already have an account, choose **Sign in** instead.

GitHub is the sign-in method supported by the current application.

![The isroot registration entry point](/images/click%20here%20to%20register.png)

## Register a subdomain

1. Open **Subdomains** in the dashboard.
2. Open the creation form, enter a name such as `myproject`, and complete the CAPTCHA.
3. Choose **Create Subdomain** and wait for the success message.
4. Confirm that `myproject.isroot.in` appears in your subdomain list.

Enter only the name, without `.isroot.in`, a URL scheme, or a path. Names must be 3–63 characters long and use lowercase letters, numbers, or hyphens. A hyphen cannot appear at either end. Reserved, inappropriate, and already registered names are rejected.

![Subdomain registration in the dashboard](/images/click%20here%20to%20register%20domain.png)

The dashboard design may differ from these screenshots.

## What happens next

Your registration lasts 365 days. Check its expiry date in **Subdomains**, and [renew before it expires](/manage-domains#renew-a-subdomain).

DNS setup is attempted during registration. If the dashboard offers **Retry setup / migration**, use it in **DNS Records** for the selected subdomain.

<Callout type="info">
The default allowance is one subdomain. Starring the repository linked in the dashboard unlocks up to five. Successful registrations are limited to one per hour. See [limits and naming rules](/limits).
</Callout>

## Connect your project

Registration does not deploy a website. Follow [Connect a project](/connect-project) to add your hosting provider's DNS records, then use [Manage DNS](/dns-manage) for future changes.
